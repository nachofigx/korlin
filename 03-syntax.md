# 03 — Syntax

*[Español disponible en docs/manual-es.md]*

## 1. Constituent order: SVO

Korlin uses **Subject-Verb-Object** (SVO) order, the most common and easiest
worldwide (Spanish, English, Chinese…).

- `mi go a le ho.` — "I go to the house."
  `mi  go  a  le  ho`
  I   go  to ART house

## 2. Modifiers BEFORE the head

Adjectives and determiners come **before** the noun (like English/Chinese).

- `le gu pe` = "the good person"
  `le  gu   pe`
  ART good person
- `me ho` = "big house"
  `me   ho`
  big  house

Order: `[determiner] [adjective] [noun]`.

## 3. Prepositions before the noun

As in Spanish/English: `a` (to), `i` (in), `de` (of/from), `kon` (with), `po` (for/by).

## 4. Negation

`na` goes **before** the verb (or copula).

- `mi na sa.` — "I don't know."

## 5. Questions

- **Yes/no**: particle `mo` at the end. `tu go mo?` = "do you go?"
- **Interrogatives**: the question word (`ki pe`, `ki lo`, `ki tem`…) goes **first**.
  `ki pe e i le ho?` = "who is in the house?"

## 6. Subordination

`ki` introduces subordinate and relative clauses. **Each clause carries its own
evidential** (each statement declares its source).

- `mi sa-ve ki li an-go-di.` — "I know that he left (I was told)."
  `mi  sa-ve    ki  li  an-go-di`
  I   know-DIR that he PAST-go-REP

## 7. Possession

With `de`: `le ho de mi` = "my house" (lit. "the house of me").

## 8. Alignment

Nominative-accusative **without cases**: subject and object are distinguished by
SVO order (subject before verb, object after). No case marking.

## 9. Final particle order

`[verb+evidential] … [epistemic] [attitude] [mo]`

- `li go-di ku bu.` — "He left (I was told, doubtful) 😤."

## Summary of rules
1. SVO order.
2. Modifier before head.
3. Prepositions before the noun.
4. Negation `na` before the verb.
5. `mo` at the end for yes/no; interrogatives first.
6. Subordination with `ki`; evidential in each clause.
7. Possession with `de`.
8. No cases (SVO order).
9. Final particles in fixed order.
