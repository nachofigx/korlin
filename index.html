✂️

Short

Words of 1–2 syllables condense concepts. ~40% shorter than Esperanto.

🛡️

Honest

Mandatory evidentiality: every statement declares its source. Lying is costly and detectable.

📱

Modern

20 letters, no diacritics, emoji particles, and AI-ready.

Example

Na-toro na-kan-sa vi i mi.

"The lie cannot live in me." · na-toro = lie (na- not + toro truth)

Why Korlin? 🚀

Built for a connected, digital, honest world.

🧑‍🎤

Made for youth

Informal, playful, emoji-native. Attitude particles express feelings with two letters that map to emojis.

🤖

AI-ready

Fewer tokens, deterministic parsing, mandatory evidentiality. Machines understand it efficiently.

🌍

Mass adoption

Total regularity, no exceptions, easy for Asian and African speakers. Learnable in days, not years.

⚡

Super modern

Digital-native: no diacritics, emoji-compatible, designed for chat, phones and AI agents.
