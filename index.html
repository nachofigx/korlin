A short, honest, modern constructed language

Welcome to Korlin 🗣️

Korlin is a constructed language designed to be short, honest and modern — a spiritual successor to Dutton Speedwords and a better Esperanto.

✂️ Short

Words of 1–2 syllables condense concepts. ~40% shorter than Esperanto.

🛡️ Honest

Mandatory evidentiality: every statement declares its source. Lying is costly and detectable.

📱 Modern

20 letters, no diacritics, emoji particles, and AI-ready.

Example

Na-toro na-kan-sa vi i mi.

"The lie cannot live in me." · na-toro = lie (na- not + toro truth)
