# Pronunciation guide — Korlin

Korlin is pronounced **neutrally**, like Spanish or Latin: pure vowels, simple
consonants and **no aspiration**. It is easy for speakers of any language,
including Asian and African ones.

## Vowels (5, pure, never diphthongized)

| Letter | IPA | Like in English | ⚠️ Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | /a/ | f**a**ther | — |
| e | /e/ | b**e**d | NOT [eɪ] as in "day" |
| i | /i/ | s**ee** | NOT [ɪ] as in "bit" |
| o | /o/ | g**o** (pure) | NOT [oʊ] as in "go" |
| u | /u/ | f**oo**d | NOT [ju] as in "you" |

## Consonants (15, no aspiration)

| Letter | IPA | Like in | ⚠️ Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| p | /p/ | s**p**in | NO aspiration (not [pʰ]) |
| b | /b/ | **b**ed | — |
| t | /t/ | s**t**op | NO aspiration (not [tʰ]) |
| d | /d/ | **d**og | — |
| k | /k/ | s**k**ip | NO aspiration (not [kʰ]) |
| g | /g/ | **g**o | — |
| m | /m/ | **m**an | — |
| n | /n/ | **n**o | — |
| f | /f/ | **f**an | — |
| s | /s/ | **s**ee | always [s], never [z] |
| h | /h/ | **h**ouse | always voiced, never silent |
| l | /l/ | **l**ow | — |
| r | /ɾ/ | Spanish "pe**r**o" | never [ɹ] nor [r] |
| w | /w/ | **w**ater | — |
| y | /j/ | **y**es | — |

## Golden rules
1. **Pure vowels**: `e` = [e], never [eɪ]; `o` = [o], never [oʊ].
2. **No aspiration**: `p t k` without a puff of air (as in Spanish/Italian/Japanese).
3. **r is a tap**: always [ɾ] (also the Japanese and Swahili "r").
4. **s is always [s]**: never [z].
5. **h is always voiced**: [h], never silent.

## Why it's neutral (easy for everyone)
- The 5 vowels /a e i o u/ are the **world's most common** system (Spanish,
  Italian, Japanese, Swahili, Hausa, Zulu, Indonesian…). Ideal for Asians and Africans.
- The tap "r" [ɾ] is the most widespread among languages.
- **No** tones, clicks, rare consonants, or consonant clusters.
- No sound requires special training: nearly any speaker already has them.
