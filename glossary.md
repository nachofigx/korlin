# Glossary — Shortlang (korlin)

> ⚠️ **This file is a convenience reference.** The **single source of truth** is
> `data/lexico.yaml` (bilingual ES/EN) + `data/afijos.yaml`. The generated manuals
> are `docs/manual-es.md` (español) and `docs/manual-en.md` (English).

Format: **word** /IPA/ · category · meaning.

## Language name
- **Shortlang** (English exonym) — official project name.
- **korlin** /ˈkoɾlin/ (endonym) — "short language" = `ko` (short) + `lin` (language).

## Grammar notes (v0.2)
- **Plural** = suffix `-s`. E.g. `li` → `lis`.
- **Yes/no question** = particle `mo` at the end.
- **Mandatory evidentiality**: every statement carries a verb suffix: `-ve` (direct), `-pen` (inferred), `-di` (reported), `-sa` (assumed).
- **Tense**: prefixes `an-` (past, from "old") and `ne-` (future, from "new"); present unmarked.
- **Derivation**: suffixes `-pe` (agent), `-lo` (place), `-re` (thing), `-i` (adjective), `-ro` (abstract); prefixes `na-` (opposite), `me-` (augmentative), `pi-` (diminutive).
- **Interrogative composition**: `ki pe` = who ("what person?"), `ki lo` = where ("what place?"), `ki tem` = when ("what time?"); they go first in the sentence.
- **Syntax**: SVO order; modifier before head; `na` before the verb; possession with `de`; subordination with `ki` (each clause carries its evidential).
