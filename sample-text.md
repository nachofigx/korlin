# Sample text — Korlin introduces itself

## The text in Korlin

> **Halo! Mi e-sa Korlin.**
> **Mi e-sa u ko lin.**
> **Mi e-sa ra ka gu.**
> **Na-toro na-kan-sa vi i mi.**
> **Mi pa-ve kon pe ka kon kin.**
> **Tu kan-ve sa mi.**
> **Ven kon mi yo!**

## Glossing (morpheme by morpheme)

**1. `Halo!`** — "Hello!"

**2. `Mi e-sa Korlin.`** — "I am Korlin."
```
mi    e-sa      Korlin
I     be-ASU    Korlin
```

**3. `Mi e-sa u ko lin.`** — "I am a short language."
```
mi    e-sa      u    ko       lin
I     be-ASU    a    short    language
```

**4. `Mi e-sa ra ka gu.`** — "I am fast and good."
```
mi    e-sa      fast   and   good
I     be-ASU    fast   and   good
```

**5. `Na-toro na-kan-sa vi i mi.`** — "The lie cannot live in me."
```
na-toro     NEG-can-ASU   live   in   me
lie         cannot        live   in   me
```

**6. `Mi pa-ve kon pe ka kon kin.`** — "I speak with people and with machines."
```
mi    speak-DIR   with   person   and   with   machine
I     speak-DIR   with   person   and   with   machine
```

**7. `Tu kan-ve sa mi.`** — "You can learn me."
```
tu    can-DIR    know   mi
you   can-DIR    know   me
```

**8. `Ven kon mi yo!`** — "Come with me! ❤️"
```
ven    with   me    ❤️
come   with   me    ❤️
```

## Free translation

"Hello! I am Korlin. I am a short language. I am fast and good.
The lie cannot live in me. I speak with people and with machines.
You can learn me. Come with me! ❤️"

## What this text demonstrates
- **Copula** `e` + evidential `-sa` (general statements "it is known").
- **Modifier before head**: `ko lin` = "short language".
- **Derivation**: `na-toro` = "lie" (`na-` not + `toro` truth, from `to` + `-ro`).
- **Negation** `na` before the verb.
- **Evidentials** `-ve` (direct) on own actions.
- **Attitude particle** `yo` ❤️ at the end.
- **SVO order** throughout.
