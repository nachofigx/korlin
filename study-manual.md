# 📚 Study Manual — Korlin (Shortlang)

**Welcome to Korlin** /ˈkoɾlin/ — a constructed language that is *"short, honest,
and modern"*: easy to pronounce, condenses concepts, makes lying costly and
detectable, and is ready for the digital age and AI.

This manual takes you from zero to speaking Korlin in 11 lessons.

## Lesson 1 — Pronunciation

Korlin is pronounced **neutrally**, like Spanish or Latin. 20 sounds, 20 letters.
**One letter = one sound, always.** No exceptions.

### Vowels (5, pure)
| Letter | Like in English |
|---|---|
| a | f**a**ther |
| e | b**e**d |
| i | s**ee** |
| o | g**o** (pure) |
| u | f**oo**d |

### Consonants (15)
| Letter | Like in | | Letter | Like in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p | s**p**in | | s | **s**ee |
| b | **b**ed | | h | **h**ouse |
| t | s**t**op | | l | **l**ow |
| d | **d**og | | r | pe**r**o (tap) |
| k | s**k**ip | | w | **w**ater |
| g | **g**o | | y | **y**es |
| m | **m**an | | f | **f**an |
| n | **n**o | | | |

### Golden rules
1. **Pure vowels** (no diphthongs).
2. **No aspiration** on p, t, k.
3. **r is a tap** [ɾ].
4. **s** always [s].
5. **h** always voiced [h].

### Syllable practice
```
ka ke ki ko ku   pa pe pi po pu   ta te ti to tu
sa se si so su   ma me mi mo mu   na ne ni no nu
ra re ri ro ru   la le li lo lu
```

## Lesson 2 — Greetings and introductions

| Korlin | English |
|---|---|
| **halo** | hello |
| **mi** | I |
| **tu** | you |
| **e** | to be |
| **nom** | name |
| **de** | of |

**Examples:**
- `Halo! Mi e-sa Ana.` — "Hello! I am Ana."
- `Mi nom e-sa Korlin.` — "My name is Korlin."

> ⚠️ **What is `-sa`?** See Lesson 6. For now: every statement carries a mark
> indicating *how you know* what you say. `-sa` = "it is known / it is a fact".

## Lesson 3 — To be and basic particles

The copula **`e`** covers "to be" (no ser/estar distinction).

| Particle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| e | to be |
| a | to, toward |
| i | in, inside |
| o | or |
| u | a, an (and number "one") |

**Examples:**
- `Mi e i le ho.` — "I am in the house."
- `Le lin e ko.` — "The language is short."

### New vocabulary
`ko` = short · `lin` = language · `ho` = house · `le` = the

## Lesson 4 — Pronouns and determiners

### Pronouns (no gender)
| Korlin | English | | Korlin | English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mi | I | | wi | we |
| tu | you | | yu | you all |
| li | he/she | | lis | they |

### Determiners
| Korlin | English |
|---|---|
| le | the |
| se | this |
| te | that |
| ke | that (over there) |

### Plural
Plural with **`-s`**: `li` (he) → `lis` (they) · `ho` (house) → `hos` (houses).

**Examples:**
- `Lis e i le ho.` — "They are in the house."
- `Se ho e me.` — "This house is big." (`me` = big)

## Lesson 5 — Verbs and tenses

Verbs **don't change for person**.

| Verb | Meaning | | Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| go | to go | | sa | to know |
| ve | to see | | pa | to speak |
| di | to say | | fa | to do |
| man | to eat | | bi | to drink |
| do | to sleep | | vi | to live |
| ha | to have | | vo | to want |
| kan | can | | da | to give |

### Tenses (prefixes)
| Tense | Prefix | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present | (none) | `mi go` = "I go" |
| Past | **an-** | `mi an-go` = "I went" |
| Future | **ne-** | `mi ne-go` = "I will go" |

> 💡 **Memory trick**: `an` = "old" (past) and `ne` = "new" (future).

## Lesson 6 — Evidentiality (Korlin's signature) ⭐

**The most important and unique feature of Korlin.**

Every statement must declare its source: you cannot say "he left" without saying
*how you know*. It is mandatory.

### The 4 evidentials (verb suffixes)
| Suffix | Source | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **-ve** | direct | "I saw/perceived it" |
| **-pen** | inferred | "I deduce it from evidence" |
| **-di** | reported | "I was told" |
| **-sa** | assumed | "it is known / assumed" |

**Examples (same sentence, 4 sources):**
- `li go-ve.` — "He left (**I saw it**)."
- `li go-pen.` — "He left (**I infer it**)."
- `li go-di.` — "He left (**I was told**)."
- `li go-sa.` — "He left (**it's assumed**)."

> 🧠 **Why**: it makes lying *costly and detectable*. Saying `li go-ve` commits you
> to having witnessed it.

## Lesson 7 — Epistemic modality (degree of certainty)

Besides *source* (Lesson 6), mark your *degree of certainty*. **Optional**
(default = certain).

| Particle | Certainty | Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| to | certain | ✅ "for sure" |
| be | probable | 👍 "I think so" |
| os | possible | 🤔 "maybe" |
| ku | doubtful | ⚠️ "I don't trust it" |

**Examples:**
- `li go-di be.` — "He left (I was told), **probably**."
- `li go-di ku.` — "He left (I was told), **doubtful**."

## Lesson 8 — Derivation (the word factory) 🧩

Don't memorize thousands of words: **derive** them from roots with affixes.

### Derivational affixes
| Affix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **-pe** | person who X | `sa-pe` = sage |
| **-lo** | place where X | `man-lo` = dining room |
| **-re** | thing of X | `man-re` = food |
| **-i** | relative to X | `su-i` = solar |
| **-ro** | quality of X | `gu-ro` = goodness |
| **na-** | opposite | `na-gu` = bad |
| **me-** | big/very | `me-gu` = excellent |
| **pi-** | small | `pi-ho` = cottage |

**Examples:**
- `sa` (know) → `sa-pe` sage · `sa-re` knowledge · `na-sa` ignore
- `toro` (truth) → `na-toro` lie ("not-truth")

## Lesson 9 — Syntax (word order)

### Basic order: Subject-Verb-Object (SVO)
`Mi go a le ho.` — "I go to the house."

### Modifier BEFORE the noun
`me ho` = "big house" · `le gu pe` = "the good person"

### Negation: `na` before the verb
`Mi na sa.` — "I don't know."

### Questions
- Yes/no: particle **`mo`** at the end → `Tu go mo?` = "do you go?"
- Interrogatives first → `ki pe e i le ho?` = "who is in the house?"
  (`ki` = what · `ki pe` = who · `ki lo` = where · `ki tem` = when)

### Possession: `de`
`le ho de mi` = "my house" (lit. "the house of me")

### Subordination: `ki`
`Mi sa-ve ki li an-go-di.` — "I know that he left (I was told)."

## Lesson 10 — Emotions (attitude particles) 😀

Like speaking with emojis. Placed **at the end**.

| Particle | Emotion | | Particle | Emotion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| yo ❤️ | joy | | pu 🤢 | disgust |
| hu 😢 | sadness | | ni 😨 | fear |
| we 😮 | surprise | | la 🥰 | affection |
| fi 🙃 | irony | | ri 😂 | laughter |
| bu 😤 | anger | | | |

**Examples:**
- `Le jo e gu yo!` — "The day is good, I love it!"
- `We! Le su e hi.` — "Wow! The sun is high."
- `Mi go a le ho fi.` — "I go to the house (irony)."

## Lesson 11 — Numbers

`u`=1 · `du`=2 · `san`=3 · `fu`=4 · `sin`=5 · `sis`=6 · `sem`=7 · `om`=8 · `nu`=9 · `des`=10

**Compose** (regular): 11 = `des-u` · 20 = `du-des` · 25 = `du-des-sin` · 100 = `des-des`

## Practice texts

### Text 1 — Korlin's introduction
> **Halo! Mi e-sa Korlin. Mi e-sa u ko lin. Mi e-sa ra ka gu. Na-toro na-kan-sa vi i mi. Mi pa-ve kon pe ka kon kin. Tu kan-ve sa mi. Ven kon mi yo!**

Translation: "Hello! I am Korlin. I am a short language. I am fast and good.
The lie cannot live in me. I speak with people and with machines. You can learn
me. Come with me!"

### Text 2 — Daily life
> `Mi an-go-ve a le ho, ka mi an-man-ve le man-re, ka so mi an-do-ve.`
"I went to the house, ate the food, and then slept." (all "I lived/knew it")

## Exercises

**1. Pronounce** aloud: `ko`, `lin`, `man`, `sape`, `natoro`.

**2. Translate to Korlin:**
a) "I go" → ______
b) "He ate (I saw it)" → ______
c) "You will sleep" → ______
d) "The small house" → ______
e) "Do you go?" → ______

**3. Translate to English:**
a) `li go-di` → ______
b) `mi na sa` → ______
c) `sa-pe` → ______
d) `le gu pe` → ______

**4. Derive** with affixes:
a) "bedroom" (from `do` = sleep) → ______
b) "food" (from `man` = eat) → ______
c) "bad" (from `gu` = good) → ______
d) "lie" (from `toro` = truth) → ______

### Answers
**2:** a) `mi go` · b) `li an-man-ve` · c) `tu ne-do` · d) `le pi ho` · e) `tu go mo?`
**3:** a) "he left (I was told)" · b) "I don't know" · c) "sage" · d) "the good person"
**4:** a) `do-lo` · b) `man-re` · c) `na-gu` · d) `na-toro`
